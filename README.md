## The creative practice

Akram works at the intersection of strategy, identity, and execution. His approach turns early concepts into clear, memorable visual systems that help brands communicate with confidence and remain consistent across every touchpoint.

The portfolio brings together identity design, art direction, campaign visuals, websites, applications, and selected brand partnerships in one focused digital experience.

## About Akram

**Akram Wanisse Marref** is an Art Director and Senior Graphic Designer based in **Doha, Qatar**. He combines creative direction with hands-on design craft, shaping projects from research and concept development through to launch-ready delivery.

Rather than treating design as a collection of isolated assets, Akram builds coherent systems: a clear idea, a recognizable visual language, and practical rules that allow the work to stay strong wherever the brand appears.

## The process

Every engagement is built around three clear stages:

1. **Research & discovery** — understand the business, audience, context, and opportunity.
2. **Design & build** — develop the direction, refine the system, and create the required applications.
3. **Launch & deliver** — prepare final assets and provide a polished, consistent result ready for use.
