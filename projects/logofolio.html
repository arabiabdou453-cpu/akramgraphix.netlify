Project Overview

This collection showcases a curated set of logos and brand marks created in collaboration with the Dzignex Studio team. Brought together under Vol.1, these designs reflect a unified approach to visual identity combining strategy, craftsmanship, and aesthetic clarity. Each logo was tailored to its brand’s personality, ensuring uniqueness, memorability, and alignment with business goals. This logofolio is not just a design showcase it’s a glimpse into the teamwork, experimentation, and creative direction that brought each brand to life.
